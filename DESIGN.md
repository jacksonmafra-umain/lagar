## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a definitive digital standard for Swedish statutory, administrative, and case law exploration. Rooted in Nordic clarity (*enkelhet, öppenhet och precision*), the identity merges the systematic rigor of Material You (M3) with the refined, tactile translucency of modern Liquid Glass materials.

The audience consists of legal practitioners, civil servants, judiciary clerks, corporate counsels, and citizens seeking authoritative legal intelligence. The visual language conveys democratic transparency, unassailable credibility, and high-density legibility without visual fatigue. Surfaces emulate frosted Nordic ice sheets—diffuse, luminous, and structured—allowing legal hierarchies to organize information naturally through precise chromatic hierarchy and strict vertical rhythm.

## Colors

The color system enforces a rigid semantic mapping tied directly to the Swedish normative hierarchy (*den juridiska normhierarkin*). Colors must never be selected arbitrarily for decorative purposes; every chromatic accent carries immediate legal weight.

### Normative Layer Hierarchy
- **Grundlagar (Constitutional Laws):** Deep Royal Blue (`#0B3D91`) paired with Constitutional Gold (`#FECC02`) for national sovereignty emblems and chapter insignias.
- **SFS Lagar (Primary Statutes):** Flag Blue (`#005B99`). The primary navigational and statutory anchor.
- **SFS Förordningar (Government Ordinances):** Light Blue (`#4A90C8`). Executive orders subordinated to primary statutes.
- **Myndighetsföreskrifter (Agency Regulations):** Deep Teal (`#0F7C7A`). Technical, administrative, and agency decrees.
- **Rättspraxis (Court Rulings & Precedents):** Burgundy (`#7A2E4D`). HD, HFD, Hovrätter, and Kammarrätter decisions.
- **EU-rätt (European Union Law):** Violet (`#5B3FA6`). Directives, regulations, and CJEU jurisprudence.
- **Regioner (Regional Decrees):** Forest Green (`#2E7D32`). Healthcare, transit, and regional ordinances.
- **Kommuner (Municipal Statutes):** Falu Red (`#8B2E1F`). Local building regulations and municipal charters.
- **Offentliga Tjänster (Public Services & Portals):** Flag Yellow (`#E0A100`). Administrative action points and portals.
- **Upphävd / Utgången (Repealed / Expired):** Neutral Grey (`#8A8F98`). Historical and defunct legal iterations.

### Text Contrast for Layer Colors
The hex values above are the `base` layer colors: icons, borders, indicator lines, tints and chart series. Six of them are too light to carry text on their own tint (Constitutional Gold, Light Blue, Deep Teal, Forest Green, Flag Yellow, Neutral Grey), so any text, badge label or cross-reference underline uses the layer's `text` token from the frontmatter instead. Every `text` token reaches WCAG AA (4.5:1) on a 12% tint of its base color over `surface`. Layer color is never the only signal: badges always carry the layer name or citation, and repealed items also carry strikethrough and an icon.

### Surface System
Surfaces are built upon a crisp Nordic white backdrop (`#f8f9fc`), utilizing subtle tints of each normative color at 10%–12% alpha for contextual panels, container backgrounds, metadata tags, and frosted glass sheet headers.

### Dark Mode
Dark mode uses the `colors-dark` roles, generated from the same seed (`#005b99`) with M3 tonal palettes. Each layer switches to its `dark` token (tone 80 of the layer's hue) for icons, borders, indicator lines and text alike; tints use that dark token at 14%–16% alpha over `surface` (`#121316`). The Constitutional Gold accent stays gold (`#f0c100`).

## Typography

Typography establishes a deliberate dual-engine structure:

1. **Systematic Shell & Navigation (`Inter`):** Handles UI chrome, metadata grids, hierarchical chips, structural menus, and search bars. Inter delivers surgical clarity, tabular alignment, and robust legibility across extreme UI densities.
2. **Statutory & Case Corpus (`Source Serif 4`):** Governs statutory paragraphs (*paragrafer, stycken, punkter*), preparatory works (*förarbeten*), and judicial reasoning. This serif font echoes formal Scandinavian publishing houses (Norstedts Juridik, Svensk Författningssamling) while providing optical comfort during sustained reading.

### Typographic Rules for Legal Text
- Paragraf numbers (§) are set in bold `Inter` to provide immediate scan anchors within long legal codes.
- Continuous legal text must maintain a line length between 60 and 75 characters (*ch*) for optimal legal scanning.
- Punctuation in statutory lists (*strecksatser* and *led*) follows indent-hung formatting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout uses a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for multi-pane legal research:

- **Desktop (1440px+):** Tri-pane workspace.
  - Left pane (3 columns, fixed 320px): Normative navigation tree, table of contents, and search facets.
  - Center pane (6 columns): The primary legal reading environment (*lagtext*), optimized for reading comfort.
  - Right pane (3 columns): Contextual metadata inspector (historical amendments, cross-references, related precedent, EU concordance).
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** Two-pane view with collapsible secondary metadata sliding in as a translucent Liquid Glass drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column linear layout. The normative tree moves to an edge-to-edge frosted bottom sheet. Sticky normative layer badges remain anchored at the top.

Outer margins dynamically step down from `2rem` on desktop to `1rem` on mobile devices. Inner paragraph spacing adheres to strict vertical metric steps (`space-sm` for intra-section paragraphs, `space-lg` between distinct § legal articles).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via **Apple-inspired Liquid Glass membranes** fused with **M3 surface layering**. Opaque drop shadows are avoided in favor of frosted translucent planes and ultra-fine light-refracting borders.

### Liquid Glass Surface Spec
- **Backdrop Filter:** `blur(20px) saturate(180%)` to render underlying statutory references as soft, ambient light.
- **Background Fill:** Translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) for high-level surfaces, dropping to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)` for sub-layers in light mode.
- **Frosted Border:** An ultra-fine `1px` continuous border set to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` on top and side edges, blending with an outer stroke of `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Specular Highlight:** Cards feature a linear ambient gradient along the top edge (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4) 0%`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 100%`) creating a delicate optical bevel.
- **Elevation Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas):* `#f8f9fc` solid substrate.
  - *Tier 1 (Article Cards & Document Body):* Translucent white (`0.85` opacity), `1px` frosted outline, 0px shadow.
  - *Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Norm Bars, Filters):* Translucent Liquid Glass (`0.72` opacity), `blur(20px)`, low-opacity ambient aura (`0 8px 32px rgba(11, 61, 145, 0.04)`).
  - *Tier 3 (Floating Overlays, Search Palettes, Drawers):* High-refraction Liquid Glass (`0.88` opacity), `blur(28px)`, diffuse shadow (`0 16px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).

### Dark Liquid Glass
Same blur values and tiers; fills switch to the dark `surface` (`#121316`):
- *Tier 0:* `#121316` solid.
- *Tier 1:* `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.85)`.
- *Tier 2:* `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.72)`, ambient aura `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.24)`.
- *Tier 3:* `rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.88)`, shadow `0 16px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- *Frosted Border:* `1px` at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, outer stroke `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- *Specular Highlight:* `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08) 0%` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 100%`.

This file is the source of truth for glass values; where other docs give different numbers, these win.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined structural rounding standard (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Default Element Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for input fields, legislative metadata chips, dropdown triggers, and buttons.
- **Card & Container Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for statutory article containers, commentary modules, case law summary briefs, and modal surfaces.
- **Liquid Overlays & Sheets (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Used for primary command dialogs, floating quick-search modals, and mobile bottom drawer handles.

Corner geometry uses smooth squircle corner smoothing (continuous curvature) to ensure glass borders align seamlessly with device viewports.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast solid fill using the appropriate normative context color (e.g., Flag Blue `#005B99` for general statute actions; Deep Royal Blue `#0B3D91` for constitutional navigation). White text, `8px` corner radius, with subtle 1px inner highlight.
- **Glass Secondary Buttons:** Translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), 1px frosted boundary, with text tinted in the relevant normative color.
- **Tertiary Buttons:** Pure text with `Inter` 500 weight, highlighting upon hover with a 10% normative wash.

### Normative Badges & Chips
- **Structure:** Pill or soft-radius chip (`8px`) identifying the authority layer (e.g., `SFS 1974:152`, `NJA 2023 s. 45`, `Prop. 2021/22:1`).
- **Coloring:** Background uses the specific layer’s color at **10%–12% opacity**, accompanied by a solid text color matching the layer at 100% opacity and an ultra-fine border at **20% opacity**.
- **Repealed (Upphävd) Badge:** Neutral Grey (`#8A8F98`) background at 12% opacity with strikethrough styling indicator and distinct historical icon.

### Legal Text Cards (§ Containers)
- Clean, modular containers enclosing individual paragraphs or statutory provisions.
- Frosted glass substrate with an ultra-thin 1px border.
- The left margin features a distinct vertical 3px indicator line tinted in the corresponding normative layer color when highlighted or referenced.
- Dedicated hover utility bar appears in the upper right, offering deep-linking anchors, history tracking, and commentary toggle.

### Lists & Hierarchical Provisions
- Legal lists format with hung indentations for numbered (`1.`, `2.`) and dashed (`-`) items.
- Line spacing retains open readability (`1.65rem`) to prevent misinterpretation of legal stipulations.
- Inline statutory cross-references (e.g., *2 kap. 3 §*) appear with a dotted underline tinted to the target layer's color.

### Input Fields & Search Command Bars
- **Main Search Field:** Translucent Liquid Glass bar with backdrop blur, 1px frosted edge, and a responsive keyboard shortcut prompt (`⌘K`).
- Focus state activates an outer glow using the primary flag blue at 15% opacity with a crisp 1.5px border.
- Filter dropdowns feature high-clarity typography with normative colored dot swatches for fast categorization.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- M3-style rounded squares (4px radius) for multiple cross-norm selection. Check mark tinted in high-contrast white over normative layer fill.
- Radio buttons for mutual exclusivity between historical document revisions (*ikraftträdandedatum*).
## Implementation Notes (Compose Multiplatform)

- **Backdrop blur:** Compose has no native backdrop blur (`Modifier.blur` blurs the content itself). Implement Liquid Glass with a backdrop-blur library such as Haze. When blur is unavailable on a target, or the user enables reduce transparency, render the tier's fill as a solid color (`surface-container-*` roles) with the same border.
- **Units:** `1rem` = `16sp` for type and `16dp` for spacing and radii. Typography must scale with the system font size.
- **Fonts:** Inter and Source Serif 4 are licensed under the SIL Open Font License; bundle them in `composeResources/font/`.
- **Keyboard shortcut:** the `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` search prompt is shown on the web target only.
- **Tokens:** generate `LagarColors` (light and dark), the layer palette and `LagarTypography` from this file's frontmatter, so values are not copied by hand.
