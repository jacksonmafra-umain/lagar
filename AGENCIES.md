# Agencies and sources

Which Swedish legal sources the corpus processes and which it does not, with the reason for each gap. Generated by `collector/agencies.py` from lagrummet.se's [rättskällor sitemap](https://www.lagrummet.se/rattskalla-sitemap.xml) (fetched 2026-10-29, 156 pages), the corpus and the collector configuration; do not edit by hand. Per-series counts are in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) and the details of each gap in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md). More agency series are tracked in [#115](https://github.com/jacksonmafra-umain/lagar/issues/115).
