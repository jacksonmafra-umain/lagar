# Agencies and sources

Which Swedish legal sources the corpus processes and which it does not, with the reason for each gap and the issue that tracks it. Generated by `collector/agencies.py` from lagrummet.se's [rättskällor index](https://www.lagrummet.se/rattskalla/) (fetched 2026-10-29, 156 entries), the rättspraxis court list, the corpus and the collector configuration; do not edit by hand. Each name links to the official page of the source. Per-series counts are in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) and the details of each gap in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md). The same data is browsable on the [landing page](https://grundlagar.vercel.app/#catalog).

68 agency regulation series, 24 agencies that publish in another agency's series, 3 of 290 kommuner configured, 17 of 23 courts with collected case law.
